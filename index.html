The story

One exhibition. Two disciplines.
A second edition.

SynthArt Synergy began as the Department of Computing's premiere art-and-technology exhibition — two days, seven working committees and a single idea: creativity is not replaced by technology, it is amplified by it.

What 1.0 built

  • An inaugural edition hosted by the Department of Computing at SLIIT Kandy UNI, Pallekele.
  • A live exhibition of digital paintings, interactive installations, VR experiences and algorithmic art.
  • A judged competition with published criteria, a judging panel and a cash-prize ceremony.
  • An industry day — ribbon cutting, oil-lamp lighting, keynote, panel discussion and awards.
  • Reach beyond campus — ten schools invited, alongside artists and the public.

What 2.0 needs from you

The second edition keeps the format that worked and raises the bar. That means a committee that can plan a calendar, hold a budget, design a gallery and keep a stage on time — not just carry a title.

Structure

How the committee is organised

Two tiers run the committee: a chairperson and vice chairperson at the top, and six management roles beneath them. Every seat is open through the application form.

01 Leadership

Chairperson

Holds the vision, the calendar and the final call on every committee decision.

Vice Chairperson

Deputises the chair and drives delivery across all seven domains.

02 Management

Secretary

Minutes, records and the paper trail between committees and the department.

Assistant Secretary

Maintains member registers, handouts and the document set for the event.

Finance Manager

Budget, purchasing and the cash-prize board.

Operations Manager

Logistics, venue readiness, technical crew and the day-of floor.

PR Manager

Invitations, school and industry contact, and outward communication.

Marketing & Media Manager

Campaign design, social media and the visual identity of 2.0.

Process

From application to onboarding

Four steps, roughly two weeks end to end. We read every submission.

  1. 01

    Submit the form

    About five minutes. Choose a primary and a secondary domain, and tell us what you would contribute.

  2. 02

    Short screening

    We check eligibility and match your interests to the domain that needs them.

  3. 03

    A short conversation

    Ten minutes with the leadership team — an interview, not an examination.

  4. 04

    Onboarding

    The 2.0 committee is published and the planning work starts immediately.

Questions

Before you apply

Who can apply for the committee?

All undergraduate students from 1st year to 4th year in the Department of Computing at SLIIT Kandy UNI are welcome to apply.

Do I need previous committee experience?

No. What matters is reliability and the willingness to own a task end to end. Most of the 1.0 team were first-timers.

Can I apply for more than one committee?

Yes — the form asks for a primary and a secondary domain. You will be placed in one of them, so it is not an additional workload.

How much time does it take?

Light for most of the planning window — a weekly check-in and your own tasks. It gets heavier in the final month before the event, which is when every committee is on the floor.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Committee registration is free. Expenses for the event itself are handled by the finance committee.

What happens after I submit?

Google Forms shows a confirmation screen and emails you a copy of your responses. Shortlisted applicants are contacted for a short conversation, and the final committee is announced by the department.
